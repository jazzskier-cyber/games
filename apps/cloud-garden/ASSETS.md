# 云上花园素材记录

生成方式：内置 ImageGen。未调用付费 API 脚本。生成后将原始 PNG 复制到项目，保留原始文件。人物与环境均为本项目的原创设计方向。

项目文件：`public/assets/cloud-garden.png`。用于 Phaser 游戏背景。

完整提示词：

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: production 2D platform game background, landscape 1536x1024. Paint a beautiful tactile gouache and watercolor storybook world called Cloud Garden. Sunlit floating botanical islands in a warm ivory and soft sage sky, layers of airy cream clouds, distant moss-covered terracotta cottages with round windows, hanging roots, tiny wildflowers and delicate floating seed motes. Sophisticated handcrafted indie game art, fine watercolor paper grain, nuanced pigment edges, atmospheric depth, warm apricot sunlight, muted celadon jade greens, dusty sky blue, natural warm earth colors. Composition: main decorative islands along far left and far right and bottom corners, center 60 percent deliberately pale open sky with very light clouds so gameplay platforms and character can read clearly over it. Wide establishing shot, gentle and adventurous, high polish. Background only, no foreground characters, no lettering, no UI, no borders, no logos, no watermark.

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: final game character cutout, genuine transparent background, one isolated full body creature centered, facing right in three-quarter SIDE view, square canvas. An original adorable traveling garden creature. Distinctive silhouette: round warm oatmeal pear-shaped body like a tiny gentle tapir crossed with a mossy pebble, two small rounded floppy ears, short wide paws, tiny curved snout with dark button nose, bright expressive dark eyes, rosy cheeks. On its back grows its OWN small lush garden mound with two broad asymmetrical sage green leaves forming an umbrella and exactly one tall apricot flower bud on a bending stalk. A small russet scarf tied at its neck flutters behind. Four short stubby legs clearly visible, softly rounded, body low and horizontal, creature is looking ahead inquisitively. Hand-painted gouache children's adventure book illustration with tactile pigment, subtle paper grain WITHIN the character only, nuanced warm shadows, carefully layered fur and leaf textures, clean readable external silhouette and premium indie game asset polish. Friendly charming slightly mischievous personality. Entire character and leaves within frame, filling about 85% of frame, feet aligned near bottom. No scenery, no platform, no shadow underneath, no text, no border, no watermark, no other creatures. Real alpha transparency outside character. NOT robot, NOT dragon, NOT Pokemon, no shell, no generic plush toy.

苔藓浮岛、绒球、阳光、晶石、花朵和粒子为项目代码绘制；页面纸纹由 SVG 滤镜生成；音效由 Web Audio 合成。没有使用第三方游戏素材。
